Behind a sign-in proxy (SSO)

With AUTH_MODE=proxy, people sign in at the proxy (for example Authelia), which tells Dispatch who they are. Clients that are not browsers cannot do that sign-in, so their paths go around it, and Dispatch checks them itself.

  • Without a proxy (password mode) none of this applies: the app has its own password sign-in; tokens and workers work the same.
  • The proxy secret matters when other programs share the proxy's address (containers on the same network).
